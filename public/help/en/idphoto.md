# ID Photo Layout

Turn a portrait photo into a print-ready ID photo sheet: crop to a template,
replace the background, and arrange one or more sizes on the paper.

## How to use

1. Add a portrait photo (drop it in or pick a file).
2. Choose a template (1-inch, small 2-inch, visa, passport, etc.).
3. Pick the print sheet (5-inch, 6-inch) and "Auto fit sheet" if needed.
4. Optional: enable "Mixed layout" and add multiple templates so the sheet
   arranges several sizes; you can also replace the background or crop the source photo.
5. Click "Generate layout", review the preview, then download the PNG or open it in a new tab.

## Cropping and background

- Cropping applies to single-template layouts: drag the photo to move and the
  corners to resize; "Reset crop" restores it.
- Background colors include white, red, blue, and light blue.

## Output

The result is arranged as columns × rows at print resolution (for example
1205×1795px @300DPI), ready for a print shop or a home printer.
