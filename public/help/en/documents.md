# Document Import

Convert PDF and DOCX files into readable Markdown for previewing, copying,
adding to a chat to discuss directly, or saving to a notebook (very long
documents are best saved to Notes first, then brought into chat).

## How to use

1. Choose or drop a PDF / DOCX file.
2. Click "Import"; the result appears in "Result preview" and "Markdown source" views.
3. Copy the Markdown, click "Add to chat" to discuss the conversion, or click
   "Save to notebook" to archive it directly.

## Use cases

- Turn papers, contracts, and meeting minutes into searchable, quotable Markdown notes;
- Combine with notebook tags and links to build a maintainable knowledge base.
