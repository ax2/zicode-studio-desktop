# Text Extraction (OCR)

Text Extraction uses the system OCR engine to pull text out of screenshots
and images; the result can be copied, downloaded, or sent to a note.

## How to use

1. Click "Choose an image" or drag a screenshot/image into the app.
2. If the text is in a language other than Chinese/English, pick the
   recognition language first.
3. Click "Extract" and the recognized text appears in the result area.
4. Copy the text, download the result, send it to the note library, or
   "Add to chat" to keep discussing the extracted content in a conversation.

## Notes

- Recognition runs entirely on this machine; images are never uploaded.
- Clear, upright text extracts most reliably; blurry or rotated text may
  need to be adjusted first.
- Common screenshot/image formats are supported (JPG / PNG / WEBP, etc.).
